# Licensing

Cairnflow is **source-available**, not open source in the OSI sense. You can read,
modify and self-host it for free, including inside commercial products. What you
cannot do is offer it to others as a hosted service that competes with ours.

> This page explains the intent of the licenses in plain words. It is not legal
> advice, and where it and a license text differ, the license text wins.

attached to every release).

## The engine license in short

The Business Source License lets you copy, modify, redistribute and use the
engine. Production use is allowed by the **Additional Use Grant**, with one
exception: you may not use it to run a **Cairnflow Hosting Service** — a service
that lets third parties (anyone outside your organization, its employees and
contractors) reach the Cairnflow APIs to register their own deployments, invoke
them, or create and manage tenants. "Cairnflow APIs" means every port, protocol
and interface the engine exposes: ingress, admin, SDK, query and console.

**Every version becomes Apache-2.0 four years after it is first published.** The
change applies per version: the release you run today will be Apache-2.0 on its
own fourth anniversary, whatever we release later.

## Examples

Allowed:

- Running Cairnflow in production for your own agents, workflows and services —
  including when they serve your customers, as long as your customers do not
  write the workflows and register them with Cairnflow themselves.
- Running it as an internal platform for your company, including multi-tenant
  mode with one tenant per team or business unit.
- Building a product on top of Cairnflow that exposes its own abstraction (a
  visual builder, a DSL, your own API), where your customers never call the
  Cairnflow APIs directly.
- Having a consultant or contractor install and operate Cairnflow for your
  organization, in your own environment: you are the licensee and they work on
  your behalf.
- Modifying the engine, forking it, and publishing your changes under the same
  license.
- Embedding the TypeScript, Python or Go SDK in any application under
  Apache-2.0 — the SDK license has no restriction of this kind.

Not allowed without a commercial license:

- A managed "Cairnflow as a service" where other developers sign up, register
  their own service endpoints and invoke them through your installation.
- Selling or giving tenants of your installation to other companies.
- Operating Cairnflow installations as a managed service for other companies —
  including one dedicated installation per customer — where those customers
  register and invoke their own deployments.

The grey zone is a platform whose customers write their own agents that then
run on Cairnflow. If your customers' code talks to the Cairnflow APIs directly,
you need a commercial license; if it only goes through your own abstraction, it
is allowed. Ask us when in doubt.

## Contributions

Code contributions from outside the project are not open yet. The engine is
offered under both the BSL and commercial licenses, which needs a contributor
agreement for outside code, and that agreement is not in place. Issues, bug
reports and design feedback are welcome. See
[`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).
